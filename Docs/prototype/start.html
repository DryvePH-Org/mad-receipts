Fill in a receipt as Sales

Choose “Sales” in the left menu. Open New receipt, tap “Fill with sample data” (or type your own), look at the Finish step, tick the check box and press “Send for approval”. Try a car that already has a deal, such as NFB 6204 (the form will tell you and offer to continue that deal).

New receipt
2

Approve it as the Approver

Choose “Approver”. Open Approvals. Each receipt shows its proof files and the balance after it. Approve one, and reject another to see the reason box. Nothing goes to Drive, Sheets, the inventory or Telegram until it is approved.

Approvals
3

Follow the deal as Admin

Choose “Admin”. Open Transactions and click a deal. A financed car, such as Bernadine Belir, has its part paid but is Waiting for lender. Mark the loan as released and watch it become Settled and the inventory change from Reserved to Sold. You can also void a receipt or cancel a deal with a reason.

Transactions
4

Check reminders and the cash report

Reminders lists overdue deals, deals due soon, loans waiting too long, and receipts waiting for approval, for whoever you are viewing as. The daily cash report adds up what each person collected by cash, check and e-wallet/bank, and how many payments have proof.

The money rules this prototype follows

The same rules are written in the Finish step of the form

  • The buyer owes us the down payment (or the whole price on a cash sale) minus the trade-in.
  • The loan is paid to us by the lender, so it is never in the buyer's balance.
  • The processing fee (7% of the loan by default, or any percent or amount) is owed to the lender, not to us.
  • A trade-in worth more than the buyer's share means we pay the buyer, recorded as a payout with proof.
  • In lieu of documents is collateral: we hold the documents, no money moves.
  • Only approved receipts count in a deal's balance.
  • Always: price = trade-in + loan + received − payout + balance.
